Features

Engineered for perfectionists

Every pixel and interaction has been crafted to feel native and instantaneous.

Time Travel

Access your clipboard history from days or weeks ago. Search instantly with fuzzy matching.

Smart Previews

Visualize colors, images, and formatted text directly in the list before you paste.

Local & Secure

Your data never leaves your device. Exclude sensitive apps like 1Password or Keychain.

Image OCR

Extract text from images in your clipboard instantly. Perfect for unselectable error messages.

Collections

Pin your most used snippets. Organize code blocks, signatures, and templates.

Native Performance

Designed for speed and efficiency. Tiny memory footprint (less than 15MB idle).
